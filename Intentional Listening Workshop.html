You can read.
But why is listening so hard?

You've studied the rules. You can read sentences.
You can speak, even people can understand you, but you just feel something is missing with your grammar, it doesn't sound natural.

You have been watching English TV shows for a while. Once you turn off the caption, you couldn't understand the program.

You have been listening to English songs. And you know the name to the song.

That's hearing. Intentional listening is different: you return to the same song or scene several times, and each time you listen with one clear goal.

What you'll learn

One clip. Four listens. Each with a purpose.

The full system, step by step — so you can practice with any song or show, any day, without a teacher next to you.

  1. 01

    Understand the context

    Who is speaking, where, and why? Learn the situation first, so the words have something to hold on to.

  2. 02

    Get the big idea

    Listen all the way through without stopping. What is this song or scene really about?

  3. 03

    Listen for details

    Replay with a mission. Catch specific words, phrases, and information you missed the first time.

  4. 04

    Study how it’s said

    Focus on pronunciation, rhythm, and how people put an idea into words — then say it yourself.
